## Protocol

Open one 30-day enrollment window at a time. The order is Deployproof, Retryglass, Revokeproof, then Rolloutproof. Recruit up to five independent repositories with an upcoming eligible change, one participant per repository. Use the pinned GitHub release and quickstart first. Track recruitment unavailable separately from product rejection.

Compare the same artifact or contract with the existing workflow. Separate cold downloads, hands-on setup, help received, and machine runtime. Follow up at 14, 30 and 90 days. Count retained use when the participant runs the check in their normal workflow on a later relevant change. Record unobserved periods separately from removal.

Record the work in the [blank private notes template](trial-template.csv). Copy it into ignored `artifacts/pilots/`. Public records contain aggregated counts and consented, sanitized evidence. Use a sanitized owner attestation for private repositories. Keep credentials, proprietary source and customer data out of reports.

## Promotion rule

Require five trials, three completed integrations, two retained uses, and two concrete benefits. A benefit is an actionable issue the baseline missed or at least 25% lower recorded hands-on setup for equivalent checks. If two participants report the same blocking problem, spend up to ten hours on one repair and recheck with those participants. If the gate still misses, maintain the alpha or prepare an upstream example.

The year-one target is five independent repositories retaining one tool, including three using it 90 days or more after integration. Track stars, forks and downloads monthly as discovery signals. Keep one active enrollment window and at most two feature tracks.
